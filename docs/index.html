How it is measured

Positions, fills, cash and the clock come from alpaca, the broker's own CLI. Realized P&L is rebuilt by matching every fill against the contract it closes, at average cost — not taken from a field.

Fees are the OCC clearing fee of 0.025 per contract, the rate the account's cash is measured to be short by.
